Privacy policy

We do not collect or transmit personal data during gameplay. Fanglight Hotel has no account system, advertising or analytics.

Game progress and preferences

The game saves your hotel and settings on your device so you can return where you left off. They remain there until you delete the app and its data. Apple device backups may include a copy, depending on your backup settings. You can manage those backups through Apple’s settings.

Visiting this website

GitHub Pages hosts these support pages. When you visit, GitHub may receive standard traffic details such as your IP address and the page you request. We do not add analytics, advertising or tracking scripts to this website.

Email and deletion requests

If you email info@spectapps.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request. You can write to the same address to request deletion of your support correspondence or to ask a privacy question.
